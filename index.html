The Master Financial Plan v4.6

Unified cross-border investment model: Remitting UK surplus to Indian vehicles.

Purpose & Assumptions

This model simulates remitting 100% of UK savings back to India to compound at Indian market rates (e.g. 12% Mutual Funds).

  • Cross-Border Compounding: UK surplus is converted to INR annually and invested in the Indian Liquid Corpus.
  • The 20% Rule (India Phase): In India, 20% of income is invested. If expenses exceed the other 80%, Liquid Investments are raided.
  • Housing: 20% Deposit in Target Year, 80% Loan at 8.5% over 20 years.
