The Master Financial Plan v4.3

A comprehensive 26-year simulation mapping Income, Expenses, Education, and Compounding Wealth.

Purpose & Assumptions

Can an Indian household afford two kids in premium international schools and a ₹2.5 Cr home purely on a domestic salary? This model compares staying in India vs. a strategic UK stint.

  • Path A: Career in India. High private school costs start immediately.
  • Path B: 1-10 years in the UK. Utilizes free state schools and GBP savings.
  • The Core Rule: 20% of income is mandatorily invested. If expenses exceed the other 80%, Liquid Investments are raided. Liquid hit zero = Fail.

Visualization Guide

Yellow Line: Education costs. Path B stays flat initially (UK school savings).
Red Line: Total Expenses. Watch for the vertical jump at the Home Purchase Year.
Dashed Orange/Pink: NPS and SSY. This wealth is locked. High "Total Wealth" doesn't help if your liquid bank balance hits zero.
