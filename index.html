Mathematical Appendix

This simulation iterates 26 annual cycles using real-world compounding logic.

1. Income & Remittance

Path B calculates a yearly surplus in GBP which is remitted to an Indian portfolio compounding at the Liquid Inv Return rate. Path A assumes a domestic career from Year 1.

Surplus = (Salary * (1 + Growth) * (1 + Bonus)) - (Living Expenses + Education Costs)

2. Liquidity vs. Locked Funds

Total Net Worth ($$NW$$) is the sum of Liquid and Locked assets. However, only the Liquid Corpus can be used to pay for the 20% home deposit or the EMI. If this falls below ₹0, the plan enters a deficit state.

Liquid Corpus_{t} = Liquid Corpus_{t-1}(1 + r_{inv}) + (Inflow - Outflow)

3. Housing Logic

Home Value ($$H$$) appreciates at 5%. EMI is calculated based on your Home Loan Interest knob.

EMI = [P * r * (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

4. Lifecycle Squeeze

Education costs escalate at 10% CAGR. The "Squeeze" occurs when the 20-year EMI starts in Year 5, just as kids enter the private schooling phase.
